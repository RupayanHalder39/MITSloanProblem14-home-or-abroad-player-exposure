# NOTICE

This repository publishes the **analytical outputs** of one MIT Sloan Sports
Analytics Conference research project. It publishes **no raw or row-level
football data**.

## What is published here

| Item | Nature | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `src/` | Analytical and modelling code, verbatim from the research workspace | Project-controlled, MIT-licensed |
| `scripts/validate_public_package.py` | Public-package validator | Project-controlled, MIT-licensed |
| `figures/` | Charts generated by this project from its own result tables | Project-generated |
| `results/` | Aggregate model-comparison tables (MAE, RMSE, correlations, ΔMAE) | Project-generated |
| `docs/`, `data/`, `paper/`, `*.md` | Documentation and availability statements | Project-controlled |

Aggregate result tables are the researcher's own measurements over third-party
inputs. They contain no player-level, match-level or row-level third-party
records.

## What is deliberately NOT published here

No raw dataset, no database, no archive, no row-level derived table, no
credential, and no private correspondence is included. See `data/README.md`
for the input specification and `PUBLIC_RELEASE_AUDIT.md` for the
file-by-file inclusion and exclusion record.

## Third-party sources and their terms

Access to a source is not a redistribution licence. The position for every
source behind this project is recorded below, as documented in the research
workspace's own source manifest.

### 1. World Football Elo Ratings — eloratings.net

- **Owner / provider:** Kirill Bulygin, eloratings.net
- **Licence as recorded:** permissive reuse policy documented by the site;
  attribution required. Redistribution of the underlying rating series was not
  relied upon and is not asserted here.
- **Used for:** the national-team Elo outcome variable.
- **Position in this release:** the raw TSV snapshots are **not** redistributed.
  Only the resulting aggregate model-comparison tables are published.
- **Attribution:** Elo ratings are by Kirill Bulygin / eloratings.net.

### 2. transfermarkt-datasets (dcaribou) — Kaggle "Football Data from Transfermarkt"

- **Owner / provider:** David Caribou (curation); underlying data originates
  from transfermarkt.com
- **Licence as recorded:** CC0 1.0 **as published by the dataset author**. The
  underlying Transfermarkt terms of service prohibit direct scraping and
  redistribution without written consent. The research workspace explicitly
  records the mirror's legal status as a grey area and advises against
  redistributing the raw files without advice.
- **Used for:** club, player, appearance and valuation tables from which elite
  club membership, elite minutes and starts are derived.
- **Position in this release:** raw tables are **not** redistributed. Neither is
  any row-level derived table.
- **This project does not scrape transfermarkt.com.** It uses the published
  CC0 dataset mirror only.

### 3. football-data.co.uk

- **Owner / provider:** football-data.co.uk
- **Licence as recorded:** free for research/private use; no explicit
  redistribution licence stated. The site was network-blocked from the research
  environment and was not bypassed.
- **Used for:** planned independent cross-check of league standings only. Not
  used in any published result.
- **Position in this release:** nothing is published. Retained in the code as a
  documented, runnable cross-check script.

### 4. StatsBomb open data and FBref / Sports Reference

- **Status:** excluded. FBref prohibits scraping. StatsBomb was documented but
  not downloaded, because its league coverage would not add coverage.
- **Position in this release:** nothing. **No claim of StatsBomb or FBref
  coverage is made anywhere in this repository.**

### 5. SoccerSolver League Analysis Dashboard

- **Data source:** SoccerSolver's own League Analysis Dashboard, the provider of
  the artefact.
- **Licence as recorded:** none documented inside the source artifact; none
  claimed by this project.
- **Used for:** club-level market-value evidence in a different research
  problem. It contributes **nothing** to the results published here.
- **Position in this release:** no SoccerSolver data is included. The
  collaboration is acknowledged as a research collaboration only; see the
  README.
- **SoccerSolver is not an author, funder, sponsor or owner** of this research.
  No evidence establishes any of those relationships, and none is claimed. The
  researcher has confirmed that the complete author list is Rupayan Halder.

> This research was developed in collaboration with SoccerSolver. SoccerSolver
> currently works with more than 10 football clubs.
